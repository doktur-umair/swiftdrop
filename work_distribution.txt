SWIFTDROP - 10-MEMBER WORK DISTRIBUTION BY VERTICAL DELIVERABLE
==================================================================
Source reference: plan.md §4 & AGENTS.md
Division Principle: Vertical delivery slices (Database + Contracts + Backend + Frontend + Tests).
Silent Work (CI/CD, Docker/Compose, Helm, Repo configs) is excluded from task complexity balance.
Target Complexity: ~20 relative units per member across DB, Backend, Frontend, and Quality/Security.
